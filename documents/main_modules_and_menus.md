# Supermarket Management System - Modules & Menus

## Module 1: Point of Sale (Checkout)
*   **Menu 1.1:** Transaction Screen (Product Entry, Shopping Cart, Totals, Quick Actions)
*   **Menu 1.2:** Payment Processing (Payment Methods, Cash/Card/EBT/WIC/Gift Card, Split Payment)
*   **Menu 1.3:** Customer Information (Lookup, Profile, Loyalty Program)
*   **Menu 1.4:** Discounts & Promotions (Apply Discounts, Coupons, Promotions)
*   **Menu 1.5:** Special Items (Weighted Items, Age-Restricted, Bottle Deposits, Lottery)
*   **Menu 1.6:** Returns & Exchanges
*   **Menu 1.7:** Receipt Management

## Module 2: Inventory Management
*   **Menu 2.1:** Product Database (Product Info, Pricing, Stock Levels, Variants)
*   **Menu 2.2:** Stock Management (Receive Inventory, Stock Adjustment, Transfer, Audit)
*   **Menu 2.3:** Purchase Orders (Create/Manage POs)
*   **Menu 2.4:** Vendor/Supplier Management (Vendor Info, Products, Performance)
*   **Menu 2.5:** Categories & Departments

## Module 3: Customer Management
*   **Menu 3.1:** Customer Database (Profile, Household Info, Purchase History, Preferences)
*   **Menu 3.2:** Loyalty Program (Profile, Rewards & Offers, Points Management)
*   **Menu 3.3:** Customer Groups
*   **Menu 3.4:** Customer Communications (Email/SMS Campaigns, History)

## Module 4: Employee Management
*   **Menu 4.1:** Employee Database (Profile, Permissions, Schedule, Time & Attendance)
*   **Menu 4.2:** Shift Management (Create Shift, Shift Board, Time Clock)
*   **Menu 4.3:** Payroll Integration (Summary, Commission Tracking)
*   **Menu 4.4:** Performance Management (Metrics, Reviews)

## Module 5: Reporting & Analytics
*   **Menu 5.1:** Sales Reports (Daily, Period, Product/Department Performance)
*   **Menu 5.2:** Inventory Reports (Stock Level, Valuation, Shrinkage, Receiving)
*   **Menu 5.3:** Customer Reports (Analytics, Loyalty)
*   **Menu 5.4:** Financial Reports (Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, Tax Report)
*   **Menu 5.5:** Employee Reports (Performance, Labor Report)
*   **Menu 5.6:** Custom Reports (Report Builder)

## Module 6: Promotions & Marketing
*   **Menu 6.1:** Promotions Management (Create/Manage Promotions, Mix & Match)
*   **Menu 6.2:** Coupon Management (Create Coupon, Digital Coupons, Performance)
*   **Menu 6.3:** Loyalty Program Management (Settings, Rewards Catalog, Communications)
*   **Menu 6.4:** Gift Card Management (Issue, Lookup, Reporting)

## Module 7: Cash Management
*   **Menu 7.1:** Cash Drawer Operations (Open, Cash In/Out, No Sale, Close/End Shift)
*   **Menu 7.2:** Safe Management (Operations, Bank Deposit)
*   **Menu 7.3:** Cash Counting (Denomination Counter, Variance Investigation)

## Module 8: Settings & Configuration
*   **Menu 8.1:** Store Settings (Information, Tax, Receipt, Payment Settings)
*   **Menu 8.2:** Hardware Configuration (Register Setup, Peripherals, Scanner/Scale Integration)
*   **Menu 8.3:** User Management (Roles, Accounts)
*   **Menu 8.4:** Integration Settings (Accounting, E-commerce, Third-Party Apps)
*   **Menu 8.5:** Security Settings (Access Control, Audit Log, Data Backup)

## Module 9: Self-Checkout (Optional)
*   **Menu 9.1:** Self-Checkout Interface (Welcome, Product Scanning, Payment, Attendant Monitoring)

## Module 10: Back Office Management
*   **Menu 10.1:** Dashboard (Today's Overview, Alerts & Notifications)
*   **Menu 10.2:** Multi-Store Management (Store Selector, Comparison, Transfers)

## Module 11: Compliance & Age Verification
*   **Menu 11.1:** Age-Restricted Sales (Verification, Reporting)

## Module 12: Advanced Features
*   **Menu 12.1:** Online Order Integration (Order Management, Curbside Pickup)
*   **Menu 12.2:** Kitchen Display System (Order Queue)
*   **Menu 12.3:** Price Book Management (Price Changes, Competitive Pricing)
